## THESIS PROPOSALS
The team is currently considering two potential research projects:

**1. Motorcycle Helmet Violation Detection and License Plate Recognition Using YOLOv8 and OCR**
This proposed project explores the use of computer vision to identify motorcycle riders who are not wearing helmets and recognize their license plate numbers.

*The system will investigate YOLOv8 for object detection and Optical Character Recognition (OCR) for extracting license plate information.*

### Main research focus:

- Motorcycle and helmet detection.
- Identification of helmet violations.
- License plate recognition using OCR.
- Evaluation of detection accuracy and processing speed.
**Proposal file:** docs/proposals/Helmet-Detection-Proposal.txt

**2. Real-Time Crowd Density Estimation in University Hallways and Canteens Using CSRNet and Computer Vision**
This proposed project explores the use of computer vision to estimate the number of people in university hallways and canteens.

*The system will investigate CSRNet for crowd density estimation and compare its performance with simpler object-detection-based counting methods.*

### Main research focus:

- Crowd image collection and annotation.
- People counting and density estimation.
- Comparison of different counting approaches.
- Crowd limit monitoring and alert generation.
- Evaluation of counting accuracy and processing speed.
**Proposal file:** docs/proposals/Crowd-Density-Proposal.txt

*The final research topic will be determined after the team evaluates both proposals.*

# PROOF OF CONCEPT
The team plans to develop a proof of concept (POC) to demonstrate the feasibility of the selected research topic.

The POC will serve as an initial implementation used to test the proposed technologies, evaluate their capabilities, and identify possible technical limitations.

Depending on the selected topic, the POC may include:
- A basic computer vision model.
- Initial dataset preparation and testing.
- Object detection or crowd counting experiments.
- Preliminary performance evaluation.
- A simple demonstration of the proposed system.

The scope and implementation details will be finalized after the thesis topic has been selected.

# USE OF AI TOOLS
AI-assisted tools may be used during the development of this project to support research, brainstorming, documentation, debugging, and understanding technical concepts.

The team will use AI tools responsibly by:

- Reviewing and verifying AI-generated information.
- Understanding the code and documentation produced with AI assistance.
- Avoiding the submission of unverified AI-generated results as actual experimental findings.
- Maintaining transparency regarding AI assistance when required.
- Ensuring that final project decisions and deliverables are reviewed and understood by the team.

AI tools will serve as development and learning assistants rather than replacements for the team's own research, analysis, and implementation.
