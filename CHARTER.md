# TEAM EARLYOUT – TEAM CHARTER

## 1. Purpose
This team charter outlines the structure, roles, responsibilities, and working agreements of Team EarlyOut for the CCSFEN1L Software Engineering course (2nd Term, A.Y. 2026–2027).

It serves as a guide for team coordination, task distribution, communication, and collaboration throughout the project.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities
- Project Lead (Scrum Master) & QA: Oversees team coordination, task assignments, project progress, and quality assurance.
- Technical Lead (Architect): Handles technical planning, system architecture, design decisions, and technical documentation.
- Research Lead & Data Lead: Manages research activities, literature reviews, dataset collection, data preparation, and research documentation.

## 4. Working Agreements
All team members agree to:

- Communicate regularly and respectfully.
- Complete assigned tasks within agreed deadlines.
- Track responsibilities and progress using GitHub Projects.
- Maintain organized and updated project documentation.
- Collaborate and assist one another when needed.
- Review work to ensure quality and accuracy.
- Inform the team about delays, concerns, or difficulties.

## 5. Backup Assignments
Designated backup members will provide assistance when the primary member is unavailable or requires support. They will help ensure that assigned responsibilities and project deliverables remain on track.

_All members are expected to actively participate, fulfill their assigned responsibilities, contribute to project deliverables, and work collaboratively toward the successful completion of Team EarlyOut's Software Engineering project._
